# 🤖 JobPulse AI

> AI-powered Job Market Intelligence Platform for discovering opportunities, analyzing market trends and understanding in-demand skills.

## 🌐 Live Application

👉 https://job-pulse-ai.streamlit.app/

## 📌 Overview

JobPulse AI is designed to combine job-market data and AI-powered analysis into a single career intelligence platform.

The platform focuses on:

* Job discovery
* Market intelligence
* Skill-demand analysis
* Career guidance
* Application tracking
* Interview preparation

## ✨ Core Features

### 🔎 Job Discovery

Search and explore job opportunities based on career domain and preferences.

### 📊 Job Market Intelligence

Analyze:

* Job demand
* Required skills
* Technology trends
* Job categories
* Market patterns

### 🧠 AI Career Advisor

AI-assisted career guidance based on the user's selected career domain.

### 📝 Application Tracker

Track job applications and application progress.

### 🎤 Interview Coach

Practice interview questions and prepare for job interviews.
